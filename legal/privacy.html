Privacy Policy — AliKa

Last updated: 17.07.2026
Publisher: ErenKa Software
Contact: alika.destek@gmail.com

1. Purpose of the application

AliKa is a transparent (non-hidden) parental control application designed to let a parent/guardian manage screen time on the device of a child in their care. The app does not run covertly: it is visibly present on the device, and lock screens and notifications are shown openly.

The app may only be installed by the device owner/guardian, with the child's knowledge. It may not be used secretly on another person's device without their consent, and such use is expressly prohibited.

2. Data collected

For its parental control functions, the app collects the following data only on the device (locally):

DataPurpose
Names and durations of used applicationsUsage reports, time limits
Titles and addresses of visited websites / videosParental visibility, content filtering
Lock / limit / block eventsEvent log
Parent PIN (stored only as a cryptographic hash)Protecting settings

3. Storage and sharing of data

  • All data is stored locally on the device only.
  • Data is never sent to any server, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • The app contains no advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • The PIN is not stored in plain text; it is hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.

(If cloud sync is added in the future, this section will be updated and separate, explicit consent will be requested.)

4. Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

This app processes data on behalf of a legal guardian, for the sole purpose of managing their own child's device. Data is not used for marketing, profiling, or third-party sharing. The guardian can delete the data at any time through the app.

5. User rights

  • Access: The parent panel displays all collected data.
  • Deletion: Deleting the app data removes all records; uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • Consent: An explicit consent screen is shown at first setup; functions do not start until it is accepted.

6. Security

Data is kept locally; settings are protected by a parent PIN. Device security (disk encryption, operating-system updates) is the user's responsibility.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this document will be updated and the change announced in the app or on the store page.
